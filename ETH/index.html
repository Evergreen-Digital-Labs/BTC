How this works. Prices are CoinMarketCap ETH daily data (peak = intraday high, bottom = intraday low; Nov 18, 2026 = CoinMarketCap live price of $1,755.70 at 3 p.m. Toronto, not a daily close). Ether has no halving, so cycles are timed from Bitcoin's halvings, which ETH's peaks and bottoms have followed. Bottom date = Aug 24, 2025 + your days; bottom price = $4,953.73 × (1 − your drawdown). Next peak date = next Bitcoin halving + your days; next peak price = projected bottom × your gain. Curves between points are illustrative paths, not forecasts.
